about selfie_nyc

NYCDOT employs the use of over 600 traffic cameras located around the city in order to monitor the real-time flow of traffic. A live feed for each of these cameras is publically available at NYCDOT's website. By scraping this feed and plotting each camera's location on a map, selfie_nyc lets users easily navigate to nearby cameras and take photobooth-style self-portraits.


This project began as a way to examine the agency afforded by taking a selfie on one's own device, and the reality of near-constant, non-consensual surveillance experienced by pedestrians throughout the city. While taking a selfie through this site may offer pedestrians a brief sense of control over how and where they’re photographed, the omnipresent eye of the city's cameras ultimately makes the collection and repurposing of our images impossible to escape. The site's colorful, retro design is intended to distract users from the reality of this panopticon, while the ability to download a photostrip keepsake represents an attempt by pedestrians to reassert ownership over their data.
