about selfie_nyc

NYCDOT operates more than 900 traffic cameras across the city to monitor the real-time flow of traffic, with a live feed from each camera publicly available on the NYCDOT's website. By scraping these feeds and plotting each camera's location on a map, selfie_nyc allows users to easily navigate to nearby cameras and take photobooth-style self-portraits.


This project began as a way to juxtapose the freedom and agency afforded by taking a selfie on one's own device with the reality of near-constant surveillance experienced by pedestrians throughout the city. While taking a selfie through selfienyc.net may offer pedestrians a brief sense of control over how and where they are photographed, the omnipresent eye of the city's cameras ultimately makes the collection and distribution of our images impossible to escape.


Visitors to selfienyc.net are encouraged to stay safe while locating cameras and to remain mindful of their surroundings and respectful of other pedestrians while taking photographs.
